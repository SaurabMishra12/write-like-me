# Observed and adopted patterns

Counts use only the four English profile prompts unless specified. These are message-level presence counts, not a population estimate. Exact examples are local-only in private/VOICE_EVIDENCE.md; constructed examples below are authored for this repository and are not personal evidence.

| Pattern | Evidence | Prevalence | Confidence | Action | Constructed application |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Direct request | A02/A03/A04 | 3/4 | Medium within prompt genre | Preserve | “Show the result and explain what it supports.” |
| Connected coverage | A02/A04 | 2/4 | Medium task preference | Preserve | Explain method, result and limitation together, removing duplicated summaries. |
| Test the interpretation | A01 | 1/4 | Low habitual evidence | Strengthen | “The score did not improve. We still need the isolated ablation.” |
| Intuitive connections | A04 | 1/4 | Low personal prose evidence | Strengthen with canon | Work a small numerical example before naming the general rule. |
| Bilingual technical thought | A05/A06 | 2/2 selected bilingual subset | Medium presence, low transcription fidelity | Preserve when supplied | Keep a user's clear technical code-switch rather than translating it unnecessarily. |
| Concrete before formal | Feynman/Shannon/Karpathy source registry | Qualitative selected passages | Medium-high craft observation | Adopt | Describe what moves through the channel before introducing its probability law. |
| Claim, reason, evidence, response | Booth/Colomb/Williams interview; source access bounded | Qualitative | Medium; not a full-book study | Adopt | Pair the measured claim with its evidence and strongest relevant limitation. |
| Accurate abstraction | Shannon 1948 | Qualitative | High within inspected opening | Adopt | Define what the model includes and what question it deliberately leaves aside. |
| Word economy | Orwell/Zinsser | Qualitative | High within inspected essays | Adopt | Replace a vague phrase with the actor and action if meaning remains exact. |
| Reader knowledge gap | Pinker 2014 | Qualitative | Medium-high inspected essay | Adopt | Define an unfamiliar term at its first meaningful use. |
| Research bottleneck | Hamming 1986 | Qualitative | Medium selected talk | Adopt | State the blocked question and the experiment that could resolve it. |
| Grounded oral explanation | Kalam/Sagan selected speeches | Qualitative | Medium; no personal speaking corpus | Adopt cautiously | Tie a technical explanation to a visible object or supplied real example. |
| Accidental typos and rushed fragments | A messages contain spelling irregularity | Not modeled | Insufficient intent evidence | Avoid copying | Correct an accidental spelling without inventing a “human” mistake. |

See research/pre2018-canon-study.md for precise primary sources, verified short excerpts, dates and access limitations. Canon excerpts are not examples of the user's prose. Do not manufacture frequency claims from selected canon passages.
