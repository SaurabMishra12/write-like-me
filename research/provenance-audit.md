# Provenance and source audit

Audit date: 2026-11-15 (Asia/Kolkata). Scope: writing-relevant chats selected from 20 recent non-pinned chat summaries and one pinned summary; no exhaustive history search claim.

## Discovery actually performed

- Writing Style searches: `professor internship`, then `internship application`. Returned research/spreadsheet materials did not establish original authorship; not retrieved into the personal corpus. Search availability is not evidence of authorship.
- Read selected chat pages through the app's read_thread tool. Assistant text, tool outputs, injected browser context, quoted course instructions, and structured master prompts are excluded from voice learning.
- Nine selected messages in five source groups are saved privately: six profile messages (four English informal prompts plus two likely transcribed Hindi-English messages), two calibration messages, one held-out message. The small English profile corpus is not representative of formal prose.
- User explicitly confirmed short informal prompts are unassisted. Confidence is high for selected English chat authorship, lower for transcription fidelity. This does not confirm attached manuscripts or long prompts.
- One source turn contained credentials. It was excluded in full; no credential-bearing content is written to this repository. No service calls use historical credentials.
- Exam-planning material and personal difficulties are excluded as unrelated sensitive context. No mental or personality characteristics are inferred.
- No private corpus or unpublished idea was sent in public research queries. Research agents received only public-source topics.
- Workspace was empty before research. Graph project `home-saurab-Documents-write-like-me`, fast generation `2026-11-14T19:41:03Z`, had Project/Branch nodes only. Coverage for scope `.` reported no recorded issues; filesystem inspection confirmed no source files. Coverage is best effort.

| Category | Admitted evidence | Use |
|---|---|---|
| A | Selected user-confirmed informal prompts; transcript fidelity qualified | Personal voice, with genre limits |
| P | Independently checked pre-2018 source passages, recorded in canon registry | Craft, not the user's identity |

| D | None individually confirmed | Rejection preferences only |
| E | Pasted master brief, structured research prompts, research-file search hits of unknown provenance | Quarantined from modeling; brief still governs requirements |

No invented B/C/D examples fill empty categories. Categories express provenance, not quality. Publication before 2018 does not by itself authenticate an arbitrary online copy or make writing exemplary.

## Privacy and control

Raw messages, identifiers, and actual excerpts live under ignored `private/`. Public profiles contain derived observations and constructed examples only. A distribution builder excludes private data and local chat IDs. Nothing has been published. Source samples can be excluded by ID; recompute evidence after exclusion. New profiles are proposals: diff them and obtain explicit user approval before replacing the permanent profile. This initial project creates a profile; it does not change a pre-existing installed skill.

## Missing evidence and import

No verified professor email, internship letter, LinkedIn post, or self-written academic paragraph was located. No email connector was used. Selected history access does not imply access to every ChatGPT conversation. Canon study depth and full-book access are reported per source in pre2018-canon-study.md.

To add data, prepare a local JSON corpus using `private/personal-samples.json` as schema: ID, category, confidence, source group, genre, split, text, and for P an original year and official URL. Import original user spans only; remove quotations and assistant expansions. Declare assistance honestly. Keep entire source groups in a single split, remove secrets before import, mark transcription, and use exclusion IDs to revoke samples. The corpus tool validates provenance, reports disjointness, and proposes a derived profile without overwriting one.
