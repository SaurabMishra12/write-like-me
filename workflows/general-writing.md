# General, informal and professional communication

Load general-profile.yaml and shared profiles. This includes LinkedIn; its finished personal register is not learned from the present corpus.

1. Identify reader, purpose and desired response. Keep an informal message in its register. For LinkedIn, request the actual work, result, evidence and limitation; do not infer achievements from an AI-written biography.
2. Separate supplied thoughts from proposed connective prose. Preserve the user's stance, genuine uncertainty and clear Indian English expressions. Do not add code-switching unless requested or already supplied.
3. Make the smallest useful edit. Correct accidental grammar/spelling without fabricating imperfection. Do not turn a clear message into a heading/list template or invent emotion, experience or significance.
   Preserve an explicit hypothetical or constructed label if the post could otherwise be mistaken for the user's real experience. A hypothetical exercise must not become a publishable autobiographical claim.
4. For Audit, return contextual findings without rewriting. For Compare, show the diff and measured properties, then explain the reader-facing tradeoff without assigning a fake voice score. For Minimal, apply an explicit edit budget and explain necessary exceptions as proposals.
5. For Learn, validate the new source's provenance, exclude B/C/D/E from voice learning, check group-disjoint splits, and produce a proposed profile diff. Never replace the permanent profile without explicit approval. Respect excluded IDs.
6. Run preservation on revisions and inspect meaning, grammar and register separately. An unchanged message is a valid result.

Acceptance: the recipient understands the same intent with no invented experience or change of stance.

Example failure test: “I helped test the prototype” becomes “I led development.” Reject the invented role, even if it sounds professional.
